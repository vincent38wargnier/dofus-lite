How to draw and export

  1. Open a guide SVG in your drawing tool, or draw over the blank PNG. Keep the canvas dimensions unchanged.
  2. Draw the same character at the same scale. Use a top-down, three-quarter isometric view. The dashed box from (32,24) to (224,224) is a suggested body area; weapons and capes may extend beyond it inside the canvas.
  3. For sheets, arrange 256 × 256 frames left to right, in a single row, without padding. Every frame has its own (128,224) foot anchor. Walking loops; casting and hits play once.
  4. Hide the guides group before export. Export PNG with transparency, without a ground shadow, tile, health bar, text or team ring. The game draws those separately.
  5. Keep the exact filenames above. Select all finished PNGs together in Atelier → Mes classes → Images du personnage → Importer les PNG. Importing replaces the previous image set.

Walking: six contact/passing poses covering a complete step cycle. Casting: prepare, anticipate, wind up, release, follow through, recover. Hits: neutral, recoil, recover. The same casting sheet is used for all spells and weapons. Optional missing poses use the idle image for the same direction.

Clothing and equipment: draw the complete outfit into these images, including any hat, cape or companion you want visible. Custom PNGs have no native Flash attachment points. Equipment statistics still apply. Death needs no image: defeated characters leave the board immediately.

What is in this kit?

  • blank/: 9 transparent canvases, ready to draw on.
  • guides/: 9 editable SVG guides. Hide the guides before exporting your PNGs.
  • example/: 9 working mannequin PNGs, to test importing and alignment. These are technical placeholders you can replace.
  • spec.json: machine-readable sizes and animation timings.
  • PROMPTS.txt: starting prompts for an image tool.

Maximum: 2 MiB per PNG, 12 MiB per set. Each animation frame must contain visible artwork; combat images must include transparent pixels. Images are stored in this browser, and used by manual combat, AI matches and replays on this browser. Keep your PNG files as a backup.
